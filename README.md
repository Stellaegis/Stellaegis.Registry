# Stellaegis.Registry

The registry of an application assembled on
[Stellaegis.Primitives](https://github.com/Stellaegis/Stellaegis.Primitives): its compartments, its users, the
catalogue of the operations its modules declare, the roles that group them, the memberships and the assignments —
what gives a right its meaning. It computes the effective rights of an actor in a space and hands them to the
decision point, [Stellaegis.Access](https://github.com/Stellaegis/Stellaegis.Access), through a port the host
implements.

⚠ Being extracted from the Stellaegis framework. Nothing is published yet; the first version will be 5.0.0.
